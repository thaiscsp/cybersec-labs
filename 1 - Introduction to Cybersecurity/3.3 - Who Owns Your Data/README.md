# ![1-up] 3.3 - Who Owns Your Data

## Objectives

Analyze a scenario involving the use of third party services and understand the consequences of accepting its Terms of Service.

## Methodology

1. Analyzed a fictional scenario where an online photo service was selected to be used by an eLearning platform for storage and sharing among departments. However, upon creating an account, the Terms of Service were accepted blindly.

## Key takeaway

Terms of Service are documents that outline the rights and obligations of parties that take part in an agreement. When you accept such terms without taking into consideration what rights you have regarding your data or what the service provider is doing to protect it, you are subject to unexpected scenarios and/or undesired consequences.

The third party service in this example could use the platform's data for other purposes, expose it, or even lose it without the employees being aware they consented to such possibilities.

That is why it's always important to read such documents and be aware of a service provider's practices regarding privacy settings, security/backup policy and how/if your data will be used by it.
